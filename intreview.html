Interview Tips

  • Research the company thoroughly.
  • Dress professionally according to the role.
  • Be punctual and well-rested.
  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Why do you want this job?
  • What are your strengths and weaknesses?
  • Maintain eye contact.
  • Smile naturally and sit upright.
  • Speak clearly and confidently.
  • Take a deep breath before answering.
  • Answer honestly, don’t bluff.
  • Practice common behavioral questions using STAR method.
  • Send a thank-you email after the interview.
  • Reflect on your performance and improve for next time.
